What does a 5-hour limit mean?
It is the maximum usage allowed within any rolling 5-hour window, measuring burst capacity. It does not equal a daily cap and cannot be scaled linearly to weekly or monthly quotas. Metering units differ across platforms (tokens / credits / requests), so align the billing unit before comparing.
