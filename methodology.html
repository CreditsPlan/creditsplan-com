METHODOLOGY

Data verification method & update cadence

CreditsPlan shows price snapshots as of a manual verification point, not a vendor’s live billing system. The "verified date" on a page is the date that record last completed a source comparison.

How prices are verified

  1. Prioritize official sources:We rely on the vendor’s pricing page, product docs, subscription page or the post-login purchase page, not third-party reposts or search snippets.
  2. Confirm the price basis:Record monthly, quarterly, annual, first-month discount, seat pricing, currency and purchase status separately; prices for different cycles are not inferred from each other, and undisclosed info is not filled in.
  3. Verify plan conditions too:Cross-check quotas, reset cycles, supported models, payment methods, invoicing and refund limits so we don’t record price without applicable conditions.
  4. Publish after review:Compare the structured record against the source page once more, update the "verified date" after confirming; anything still ambiguous is marked "pending" or "see official site".

How to read the fields

Verified date
The date this plan was last manually compared against official sources, not the vendor page’s publish date.
Price history
Currently we archive only monthly price and currency. After each verification, a record is added if the monthly price changed; stable periods also keep monthly checkpoints.
Pending / see official site
Means official info is missing, unclear, or not yet re-reviewed. Before buying, open the official source to confirm live price and billing terms.

How often it updates

Listed plans are in principle re-reviewed at least once every 30 days. We review earlier when a vendor changes prices, plans go on/offline, rules change, or on valid feedback.

Data is not synced in real time. Whether it is updated depends on the "verified date" next to each plan.

Found an error?

Please include the plan name, official page link, the price or rule you saw, and the date you found it, so we can re-verify quickly.
